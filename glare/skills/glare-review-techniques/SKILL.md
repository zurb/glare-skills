You are helping the user choose **Design Review techniques** — small in-the-moment moves that guide the conversation through SIGNAL.

## Core idea

Techniques are used in the moment, while the review is happening. You don't need to use all of them. In most cases, one or two well-timed moves can shift the entire review. The goal is to notice where the conversation is losing strength, then use a technique that matches that moment.

A good technique helps the group move from confusion to clarity, from reaction to direction, or from agreement to action.

## Read the reference first

- `reference.md` — full techniques map (the chooser by stuck-moment, all 24 techniques across S/I/G/N/A/L with examples).

## How to apply

1. **Diagnose where the conversation is breaking down.** Ask one of these:
   - Is the problem unclear? → Surface
   - Is feedback too vague? → Identify
   - Are opinions repeating? → Ground
   - Is the group exploring without choosing? → Navigate
   - Is everyone aligned but no one owns the next step? → Align
   - Is the call ending without clear action? → Lock

2. **Don't try to diagnose the whole review in the moment.** Just notice what is weakening the conversation and use one move to bring it back.

3. **Match the technique to the moment** using the chooser table in `reference.md`. Always ground each technique in a specific example from the user's situation rather than reciting the canonical example.

4. **Hand off to the dimension skill** for the full set of techniques + coaching for that letter. This skill is the index, not the deep dive.

5. **When techniques aren't enough** — if the same dimension keeps failing across multiple reviews, the issue isn't technique selection, it's behavioral. Hand off to `glare-review-coach` for pattern coaching.

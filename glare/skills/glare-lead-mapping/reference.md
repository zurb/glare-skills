# Lead — Mapping (verbatim extract)

## Glare | Show, Mapping v1.0

Mapping draws a line from user needs (bottom) to business goals (top), turning outputs into outcomes. Executives care about outcomes, not outputs.

**Three Layers of Metrics** (with example outcomes):
- **Design KPIs** — completion rate, error rate, comprehension, effort, sentiment. *Ex: 85% of users completed onboarding without error.*
- **Product KPIs** — DAU/WAU, adoption rate, retention, feature usage, engagement depth. *Ex: 40% increase in trial-to-paid conversion.*
- **Business KPIs** — revenue, churn, LTV, CAC, ROI, cost savings, market share. *Ex: pipeline revenue grew by $2M.*

UX metrics are the **connective tissue**: they make product metrics explainable ("retention dropped because comprehension fell") and business metrics credible ("revenue grew because adoption increased, fueled by higher task success"). Without UX metrics leaders only see lagging indicators.

**The Mapping Path** (5 rungs, verbatim):
1. **User Needs** — what people are trying to get done
2. **User Outcomes (Design KPIs)** — can they succeed in the moment?
3. **Product Outcomes (Product KPIs)** — do they adopt and return?
4. **Business Outcomes (Business KPIs)** — does the company benefit?
5. **Business Goals** — pressures every company faces (growth, retention, efficiency, operations)

**How to Use This Map**:
- Work upward: tie back to the user need (talk to UX research, customer success, support).
- Work downward: connect Design KPI → Product KPI → Business KPI (talk to PMs, analytics, finance).
- Check alignment: if you can't connect a Design KPI to a business outcome, the signal won't resonate.

**Case study — Onboarding → Revenue** (the recurring example): User Need = "finish onboarding quickly"; Design KPI completion went 55% → 85% after redesign; Product KPI trial-to-paid conversion doubled; Business KPI pipeline +25%; Business Goal = revenue growth.

**Quick Exercise**: pick one feature this month and walk it up the ladder (need → design KPI → product KPI → business KPI → business goal).

## Glare | Show, Mapping v1.1

A more procedural, training-style rewrite of v1.0 with explicit steps and tables. Mapping is positioned within the **Lead facet** of Glare. It "closes the loop between what teams learn and what leadership values."

**Section relationships** (Business Goals = north star; Workflows = data/ownership flow; Mapping = ladder of outcomes; Results = visualize and share progress in business language).

**Five-rung Chain of Proof**: User Need → User Outcome (Design KPI) → Product Outcome (Product KPI) → Business Outcome (Business KPI) → Business Goal.

**Five Steps to Map**:
1. **Align on Business Goals** — pressures: Growth, Retention, User Experience, Efficiency, Operations. Each pressure has example goals (same 20 goals from the Business Goals doc).
2. **Understand the Workflows** — same eight functions and influence: Sales, Marketing, Product, Engineering, Strategy, Operations, Finance, Legal.
3. **Map the Chain of Proof** — fill each row of the ladder with question/example.
4. **Share Across Teams** — review with cross-functional owners; ask which metrics moved, where design created lift, how signals can enter decision cycles earlier.
5. **Keep It Visible** — embed in dashboards, OKR reviews, roadmap decks. Mapping is a living process, not a one-time deck.

**Make It Practical — five-step routine**: Define the Goal → Connect the Workflow → Build the Chain → Share the Story → Refresh the Data (quarterly or after each launch).

**Quick Checklist**: defined business goal? UX metrics connected to product/business KPIs? chain visible to every function? story refreshed with new signals?

**Worked example** (verbatim 5 steps): trial-to-paid conversion problem — Goal: Growth → Workflow: Product+Sales → Design KPI: usability (55% → 85% onboarding completion) → Product KPI: trial-to-paid doubled → Business KPI: pipeline +25%. Lesson: a single usability signal becomes business proof when mapped end-to-end.

**Reflection questions**: Did we define business goals before measuring? Does the map clearly connect UX → product → business KPIs? Is the map visible across teams? Have we refreshed since last review?

## Related key terms

- **Chain of Proof** — the five-rung ladder: User Need → Design KPI → Product KPI → Business KPI → Business Goal.
- **Connective tissue** — recurring metaphor for UX metrics linking user behavior to business outcomes.
- **Design KPIs / UX metrics** — completion rate, error rate, comprehension, effort, sentiment, time on task.
- **Product KPIs** — adoption rate, retention, engagement depth, feature usage, DAU/WAU, trial-to-paid conversion.
- **Business KPIs** — revenue growth, churn, LTV, CAC, ROI, cost savings, margin, market share.
